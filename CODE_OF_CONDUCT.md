# Code of conduct

We want this project to be a welcoming, harassment-free place to ask questions,
report problems and contribute, whatever your experience, background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Keep discussion on the work: concrete requests, reproducers and evidence.
- Respect privacy. Do not post other people's personal information, credentials
  or private source code.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that violate
this code, and may restrict participation. To report a concern, use GitHub's
[private reporting](https://github.com/Flambeau-Phoenix/butter-modified-config/security/advisories/new)
or contact a maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled
confidentially.

This code applies in the project's spaces and when representing the project in
public. It is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1
(https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
